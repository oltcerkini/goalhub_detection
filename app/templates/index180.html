GoalHub 180

VR180 · upload → calibrate → detect players

1Upload a 180° video

2Trace the pitch with the pen

Pick Pitch, then click the four corners (top-left → top-right → bottom-right → bottom-left). Plain clicks stay straight; drag while clicking to curve just that edge. Afterwards, drag any line to bend it in place — no new point. Add Halfway and Centre circle if visible (the centre circle helps most), then press Calibrate.

3Detect players + ball

Only people whose feet are on the traced pitch are kept; the ball is tracked as a moving trail.
